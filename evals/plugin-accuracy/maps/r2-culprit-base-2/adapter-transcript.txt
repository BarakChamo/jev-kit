Summary of what `adapter.ts` does:
- `buildState`/`questions` both call `decide.ts`'s own `buildRequest(jobId, input.log_lines.length, input.log_lines)` and return its `.state` / `.questions`, so they stay byte-for-byte consistent with `questions.json`'s shape.
- `decide` converts the API's `noul` answer (`{type, noul}`) into the plain probability number `decide.ts` expects (`noul ? 1 : 0`), calls `decide.ts`'s `decide()`, then maps its `Decision` back to `{ culprit_lines: number[] }` by subtracting `state.start_line` off each returned `lineNumber` to recover 0-based indices into `input.log_lines` — empty for `"infra"`, one line for `"pinpoint"`, the ordered shortlist for `"ambiguous"`.
