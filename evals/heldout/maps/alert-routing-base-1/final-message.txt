Wrote `map.mjs`. It asks Jev two questions per alert: which catalog service is actually affected (choice, with a "none" option for ambiguous cases) and the alert's severity (critical/high/other). `decide` then applies the policy deterministically — team from the catalog lookup, page_now = yes if critical, yes/no by tier if high, no otherwise — and abstains on team or page_now independently whenever the relevant answer's confidence is below threshold or the service can't be matched.
