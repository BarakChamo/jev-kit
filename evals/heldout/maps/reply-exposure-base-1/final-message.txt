Created `map.mjs` with a single `noul` question asking Jev whether the draft names, emails, phones, or order-ids a specific person other than the recipient — explicitly excluding the recipient's own listed identifiers, company contact info, and staff first names. `decide()` maps probability ≥0.65 → `"yes"`, ≤0.2 → `"no"`, and the uncertain middle band (plus missing/malformed answers) → `"abstain"` for human review, given the high stakes of millions of replies a month.
