Built `map.mjs` with four scoped `noul` questions (one per data type: name, email, phone, order id), each excluding the recipient's own data and the carve-outs (company contact addresses, support staff first names). `decide()` flags `"yes"` if any category is confidently positive, `"no"` only if all four are confidently negative, and `"abstain"` otherwise — biasing toward caution given the exposure risk.
