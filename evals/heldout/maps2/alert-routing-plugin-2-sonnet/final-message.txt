Design:
- **`affected_service`** — one `choice` over all catalog services (plus `unclear`), rubric explicitly distinguishes the alert's own metric target from services only mentioned as context/dependency (rule 11: pick-one-of-many via a single `choice`, not a `noul` per candidate).
- **`severity`** — one `choice` over `critical/high/other/unclear`, scoped to what the alert text itself states (avoids conflating stated severity with perceived badness).
- **`decide`** derives team, tier, and the page/ticket policy purely in code from the catalog lookup (rule 12) — Jev is never asked to compare or apply the policy itself.
- Gates on the **probability of the chosen label** (not the raw `confidence` scalar, per rule 8) plus an explicit `unclear` option (rule 9); either low confidence or `unclear` abstains that field, and `page_now` abstains whenever team or severity isn't resolved, since applying the policy without both would be a guess.
