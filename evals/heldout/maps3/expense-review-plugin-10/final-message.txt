**Design summary** (`map.mjs`):

- `buildState`: passes `policy_text` and the full `expense` object — no derived facts, so Jev always sees the actual source text (rule 1).
- `questions`: 3 questions total —
  - `category_limit` (choice, ~95-value USD grid + `no_limit_stated`/`greater_than_1000`): reads the exact per-unit limit for this line's category+city from the policy, rather than banding it (rule 5's dinner/$73.44 failure mode).
  - `receipt_threshold` (same grid): reads the itemized-receipt threshold.
  - `never_reimbursable_item` (noul): uses the "does it *include* X, even as part of a larger purchase" framing rather than "is it a kind of X" — the framing the skill's rules cite as the fix for this exact expense-policy failure mode.
- `decide`: does all arithmetic in code — FX conversion, and the limit/threshold comparisons — never asks Jev to compare quantities. Gates on the top choice probability (≥0.6) and on the noul's two boundaries (≥0.8 reject, ≤0.2 clear); anything in between, or a missing FX rate, returns `"abstain"`. A category/city with no stated limit routes to `"needs_approval"` (a safe deterministic default), not abstain.
