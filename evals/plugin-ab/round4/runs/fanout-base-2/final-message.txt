Created both files in the working directory:

- **`questions.json`** — the full Jev request: example `state` (contract metadata + document text) plus 25 `questions`, split across all three types: 15 `noul` (booleans like liability cap present, auto-renewal, data processing terms), 4 `choice` (unordered categoricals: governing law, indemnity scope, dispute resolution, IP ownership), and 6 `score` (ordinal bands: renewal notice, liability cap amount, termination notice, cure period, payment terms, confidentiality term).
- **`decide.ts`** — post-processing logic: normalizes `noul` probabilities to booleans with a confidence band, normalizes `choice`/`score` answers by their `confidence` threshold, flags any low-confidence field for manual review, and adds a few cross-field risk checks (auto-renewal trap, uncapped/one-sided liability, broad indemnity, missing data-processing/breach terms) that drive a `needsManualReview` flag — useful at 40k contracts/year to route only the exceptions to a human instead of every contract.
