**`questions.json`** — 10 policy-category `noul` questions (hate speech, threats, harassment, adult/minor sexual content, self-harm, illegal goods, scam, doxxing) plus a `reviewer_manipulation` detector and a `context_sufficiency` `choice`, each scoped to named state fields (`post_text`, `media_transcript`, `reply_context`) with an illustrative example state.

**`decide.ts`** — thresholds and the remove/escalate/allow logic, kept out of Jev entirely: per-category remove/escalate probability gates, a zero-tolerance path for minor sexual content, and two overrides that force escalation rather than allow — insufficient context and detected reviewer-manipulation attempts.

One architectural point worth flagging: the "LLM turns the policy into questions" step should happen **offline**, not per-request. When the policy team edits the doc, an LLM compiles a new draft of `questions.json`, you score a few compiled versions against ~30 labelled posts, and pin the winner — never recompile live per post. Millions-of-posts/day traffic then hits the same pinned question set until the next policy edit. I left a comment to that effect at the top of `decide.ts`; before shipping, the `jev:jev-eval` skill covers building that labelled set and auditing confidently-wrong answers, and thresholds in `GATES` should be tuned against it.
